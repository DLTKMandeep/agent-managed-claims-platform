# Stage 1: Intake (Intent)

**Phase:** Inception
**Status:** Approved by the manager on 2026-11-24

## Intent

Build a prototype claims processing platform run entirely by AI agents, to show leaders what it means to be a "manager of agents."

## In scope

Six worker agents (intake, validation, clinical review, medical coding, pricing, submission), an orchestrator, a mock insurance company, a manager's dashboard with per-agent performance reviews, and a before-and-after comparison with the manual process.

## Out of scope

Real patient data, real insurers, real billing rules, and production-grade security or compliance.

## Data

Synthetic records only, including deliberately messy cases that trigger escalations.

## Success looks like

A viewer can watch claims flow through, see an agent escalate to the manager, and open any agent's scorecard showing goals, KPIs, false positive and false negative rates, skills and improvement areas.

## Framing

The story is people moving up from routine checks to judgment and oversight, not people being replaced. The platform always has a human manager accountable for results.
